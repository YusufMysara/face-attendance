      What is this?

      Face Attendance is a demo of an attendance system that uses face recognition. Point a webcam at yourself and it works out who you are and records that you are present for the day. People who are enrolled but have not been seen yet are absent.

      It is a backend and AI portfolio project: a web server, a database and a face-recognition model working together, with this page as the control panel.

      Try it in four steps
      1. Click Try Admin at the top right. There is no password; it only shows the Employees and Attendance tabs.
      2. Open Employees, type your name, face the camera and click Capture & enroll. One photo is enough.
      3. Go to Live. Your face gets a green box with your name. The first time you are seen on a day, you are marked present.
      4. Open Attendance. You appear under Present with the time you were first seen.
      How recognition works
      1. Camera
      2. Server
      3. Face found
      4. 128 numbers
      5. Compared
      6. Name

      Up to 4 times a second, this page sends a picture from the camera to the server. A detector (YuNet) finds the faces. A second model (SFace) turns each face into a list of 128 numbers, a kind of "face print". Faces of the same person give similar numbers.

      The database (PostgreSQL with pgvector) looks for the stored face print that is closest to the new one. If it is close enough, the person is recognized and, if it is their first sighting of the day, attendance is recorded. If not, the face is shown as "Unknown".

      What the colors and numbers mean

      A green box is a recognized employee. An orange box is a face the system does not know.

      The number next to the name is the match score, between 0 and 1. A score of 1 means identical face prints, and around 0 means unrelated faces. The system accepts a match at 0.40 or higher. In the author's tests with two people, the same person usually scored 0.6 to 0.9 and different people about 0.1 to 0.3.

      What is stored

      Only the person's name and their 128-number face print. Your photo and the camera pictures are analyzed and then discarded; they are not saved in the database.

      Delete on the Employees tab removes a person, their face print and their attendance records. Clear all data removes everyone.

      Limits to know about
      • A photo of someone held up to the camera can fool it. There is no check that the face is a real, live person.
      • There is no login. "Try Admin" only shows tabs, and anyone who can reach the server can use every function.
      • One clear match is enough to mark someone present.
      • Accuracy was checked on a small test. Do not use this for real security or payroll.
      Under the hood

      Built with FastAPI (Python), OpenCV (YuNet and SFace models), PostgreSQL with pgvector, and plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript for this page. It runs in Docker.

      Tips for a good photo
      • Face the camera and keep your whole face in view.
      • Use even light: a window in front of you, not behind you.
      • Make sure you are the only person in the picture.
      • Take off anything that covers your face.

      The photo is turned into 128 numbers and then discarded. The same face cannot be enrolled twice.

          Welcome to Face Attendance

          This is a demo of an attendance system that recognizes faces through your webcam. When someone enrolled looks at the camera, they are marked present for the day.

          1. Click Try Admin (top right).
          2. Open Employees and enroll yourself with one photo.
          3. Go back to Live and look at the camera.

          Your camera pictures are analyzed by this server and are not saved. Only a name and 128 numbers are stored for each enrolled person.
